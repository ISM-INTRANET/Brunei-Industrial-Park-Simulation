How the sandbox calculates results

These are deliberately simple planning assumptions, not measured Brunei sector averages. Tenants are split evenly across selected sectors. Each sector has fixed per-tenant land, utility, trip and job factors. Jobs are reduced by the tightest land, utility, plot-area or budget constraint, then by zoning and missing critical services. All scores are directional.

  • Developable land = land area less green area and an assumed 18% for internal roads and common space. Plot area is calculated from each editable footprint using a conceptual 1.296 ha per map-square scale.
  • Brownfield means previously developed land; greenfield means previously undeveloped land. These shares add to total site area and are separate from planned green space. The brownfield site-preparation allowance is illustrative and does not replace a contamination survey.
  • Infrastructure resilience rewards spare capacity and shared services; sustainability reflects green area, wastewater headroom, resource pressure, and sector mix.
  • Capital estimate uses simple unit costs for site preparation, installed utility capacity, roads, fibre and selected services. Overbuild is flagged above roughly 180% of demand.
  • Annual payroll proxy assumes BND 32,000 per modelled direct job; it is not GDP, tax revenue, export value, or an official forecast.
